About

Playa Development.

A father-and-son studio for luxury trim carpentry in South Florida, led by Michael Entenza Sr. and Michael Entenza Jr.

The work is the fit: coped joints that disappear, shadow lines that hold, and profiles that belong to the house. Every piece is considered against the light of a coastal interior—salt air, high sun, and rooms that ask for restraint as much as ornament.

Obsession with finish is the brief. Nothing is left proud of the plane that shouldn’t be.

The studio

Trim carpentry as the discipline.

Playa Development is a father-and-son company, not a general contractor. Michael Entenza Sr. and Michael Entenza Jr. run the practice: ceilings, casings, crown, and interior doors, fitted to houses that ask for the same care as the architecture.

There is no catalog of packages. Each house is measured. Each profile is chosen. Each joint is considered against the light.

Approach

How work begins.

01

The house

A conversation about the rooms, the existing millwork, and the light. Scope is written to the wall, not to a SKU list.

02

The profile

Casing, crown, ceiling, and eave are chosen as a family. Returns, copes, and grain direction are decided before the first cut.

03

The fit

Pieces are scribed, joined, and finished so the millwork sits in the architecture. The last pass is the plane itself.
