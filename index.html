Craft

Three disciplines. One standard of fit.

Work is measured to the wall, not the catalog. Profiles are chosen for the room’s light, scale, and existing millwork.

01

Tongue-and-groove ceilings

Patio, porch, and soffit tongue-and-groove. Boards run true, edges closed, and lights cut clean so the plane holds.

02

Custom casings & crown

Door and window casings, headers, and crown run as a single composition. Returns, copes, and miters finished so the profile never breaks at a corner.

03

Interior doors & casing

Interior doors hung plumb, margins even, then cased. The swing and the wrap are the same job.

About

Playa Development.

A father-and-son studio for luxury trim carpentry in South Florida, led by Michael Entenza Sr. and Michael Entenza Jr.

The work is the fit: coped joints that disappear, shadow lines that hold, and profiles that belong to the house. Every piece is considered against the light of a coastal interior—salt air, high sun, and rooms that ask for restraint as much as ornament.

Obsession with finish is the brief. Nothing is left proud of the plane that shouldn’t be.
